## What This Is

Complete AAISM exam preparation material covering the full syllabus across three domains:

| Domain | Weight | What It Covers |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **I · AI Governance & Program Management** | 31% | Stakeholders, strategy, policy, asset management, program operations, incident response |
| **II · AI Risk Management** | 31% | Risk assessment & thresholds, threat landscape, vendor & supply chain risk |
| **III · AI Technologies & Controls** | 38% | Architecture, lifecycle controls, data controls, privacy & ethics, monitoring |

Every chapter explains *why* each control exists — so the material survives the exam and remains useful afterwards.

## Quick Start for CISM / CISSP Holders

The exam assumes you already think in governance, risk appetite, and control design. What it adds is a working mental model of how AI systems are built, where they break, and why your existing security instincts sometimes give the wrong answer.

- **Short on time?** Start with Domain 3 (38% of the exam, least covered by CISM/CISSP)
- **Comfortable with governance?** The first third will feel familiar — don't let that lull you
- **Study plan in Chapter 30** — six weeks, weighted to the exam
