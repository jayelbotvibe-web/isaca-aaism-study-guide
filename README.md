# Securing the Machine · AAISM study guide

An independent study companion for **ISACA Advanced in AI Security Management (AAISM)**. Edition 3 · v3.1.

**[Read online](https://jayelbotvibe-web.github.io/isaca-aaism-study-guide/)** or download [index.html](index.html) and open it in your browser.

## Study material

- **31 chapters** covering AI foundations, governance, risk management, technologies and controls.
- **42 diagrams** explaining concepts, decisions and workflows, with text alternatives.
- **90 original practice questions** with explanations for every answer choice.
- **93 recall questions** and a continuing Calder Manufacturing case for applying each chapter.
- A **13-topic diagnostic**, timed practice and a six-week study plan.
- A glossary, case index, references and a map connecting the material to the 13 public subtopics and 22 supporting tasks.

| Area | Topics |
|---|---|
| Foundations | Models, task selection, data, lifecycle and deployment |
| Governance | Roles, policy, inventory, program management and incident response |
| Risk management | Assessments, threats, treatment, vendors and supply chains |
| Technologies and controls | Architecture, agents, release gates, privacy, explainability and monitoring |
| Frameworks and preparation | Standards, regulation, study planning and practice |

## How to study

1. Try the 13-topic diagnostic before revealing the explanations.
2. Read the foundations, then use the coverage map to study your gaps.
3. Apply the chapter cases and answer recall questions from memory.
4. Attempt the practice questions and explain why each alternative fits or fails.

The guide works offline, saves progress in your browser and supports printing. Use **Continuous** to search the whole book or **Enlarge** to inspect a diagram.

## Scope and sharing

The material follows the [public AAISM exam outline](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aaism/aaism-exam-content-outline). Chapter mappings, practice questions and fictional examples are independently authored. Practice scores do not predict exam results. Check current exam requirements and legal obligations with the linked primary sources.
